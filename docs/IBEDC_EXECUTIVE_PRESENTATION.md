# CEPHAS PowerOps — IBEDC Executive Presentation

## Slide 1 — Title
**CEPHAS PowerOps**  
Enterprise Electricity Distribution Intelligence  
*An interactive Proof of Value proposal for Ibadan Electricity Distribution Company.*

## Slide 2 — The operational opportunity
Distribution utilities manage large volumes of feeder, transformer, outage, meter, billing and asset information. PowerOps is designed to bring approved data into one explainable operational intelligence environment.

This is an opportunity statement, not a claim that IBEDC currently has a specific data-quality or performance problem.

## Slide 3 — What PowerOps is
An advisory intelligence platform for:
- Feeder performance and capacity indicators.
- Transformer loading and maintenance prioritisation.
- Outage frequency, duration and recurrence analysis.
- Energy-input and billed-energy reconciliation.
- Demand trends and forecasting where data supports it.
- Executive operational reporting and evidence-grounded AI assistance.

## Slide 4 — Interactive command centre
Show the executive overview, feeder explorer, transformer explorer, outage analysis, energy reconciliation and evidence panel. Every demonstration view must show the synthetic-data banner.

## Slide 5 — Five live demonstration scenarios
1. Identify feeders with the highest loading indicators.
2. Prioritise transformers for human investigation.
3. Explore recurring outages and restoration duration.
4. Investigate energy reconciliation gaps without unsupported loss claims.
5. Ask evidence-grounded operational questions using the selected dataset.

## Slide 6 — How the intelligence works
Approved source data → data validation and lineage → deterministic feature engineering → evaluated ML models where appropriate → explainable recommendations → authorised human review.

Measured facts, derived features, model outputs and engineer-validated conclusions are kept separate.

## Slide 7 — Data and integration approach
Initial demonstration: synthetic records only.  
Customer validation: approved extracts from IBEDC systems, subject to discovery and security approval.  
Potential source categories: GIS, SCADA/ADMS/DMS exports, AMI/MDMS, meter and billing records, outage logs, asset registers, inspections and work orders. No integration is presumed live.

## Slide 8 — Security and operational safety
Customer-controlled deployment model; database-enforced tenant and business-unit isolation; role-scoped access; auditability; signed annual CEPHAS licensing as a target capability subject to implementation and validation. PowerOps is advisory-only and does not issue network-control commands.

## Slide 9 — PoV success measures
Data coverage and quality, indicator reproducibility, forecasting error where applicable, detection precision/recall where labels exist, outage reconciliation, analyst acceptance, investigation time, stability and security evidence. Baselines and targets are agreed with IBEDC.

## Slide 10 — Proposed engagement
1. Executive and technical discovery.
2. Agree PoV scope, data owners and acceptance criteria.
3. Demonstrate the synthetic interactive environment.
4. Validate approved IBEDC sample data in a controlled environment.
5. Present findings, limitations, integration plan and commercial deployment proposal.

## Slide 11 — What IBEDC receives
Interactive demonstration; data-readiness assessment; scenario evaluation; technical architecture; security and integration gap register; KPI evaluation report; proposed implementation roadmap and commercial proposal.

## Slide 12 — The request
Nominate executive and technical sponsors, agree a discovery workshop, identify candidate data owners and approve a controlled sample-data validation process.

**Closing:** CEPHAS PowerOps — Better visibility. Earlier investigation. Evidence-led distribution operations.

## Presenter disclosure
All initial screenshots, charts, records, alerts and results generated from the offline fixture must be labelled synthetic. Do not present simulated connections, performance gains, model accuracy or customer outcomes as measured facts.
