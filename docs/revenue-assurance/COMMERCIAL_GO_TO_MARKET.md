# PowerOps Revenue Assurance — Commercial & Go-to-Market Plan

## Positioning

**CEPHAS PowerOps Revenue Assurance Agent** is an evidence-led revenue protection platform for utilities and infrastructure operators. It finds and explains revenue exceptions, coordinates human investigations and tracks verified recovery. It is not sold as a generic chatbot or as an autonomous enforcement tool.

Core commercial message: **turn fragmented meter, billing, vending and payment data into traceable revenue assurance cases and measurable, verified recovery.**

## Initial ideal customer profile

1. Electricity distribution utilities with material customer, metering, billing and collections operations.
2. Electricity retailers, municipal utilities and water utilities with meter-to-cash processes.
3. Telecoms and other metered infrastructure operators after the utility edition is validated.

Buying group:
- Economic sponsor: CEO, CFO, COO or Chief Commercial Officer.
- Operational champion: Head of Revenue Assurance, Commercial Operations or Metering.
- Technical approvers: CIO/CTO, CISO, Enterprise Architecture, Data/IT.
- Control stakeholders: Internal Audit, Legal, Privacy/Data Protection and Procurement.

## Beachhead and expansion sequence

1. IBEDC design-partner PoV.
2. Other Nigerian DisCos, using reusable data contracts and configurable utility hierarchy.
3. Selected African utilities where local tariff, currency, data residency and market rules are configured.
4. International utilities and municipal operators through local systems integrators and utility technology partners.
5. Adjacent metered sectors only after the electricity revenue assurance product is validated.

Do not claim a customer partnership, connector, production deployment or measured recovery until documented and accepted.

## Pricing model

Use annual software licence as the commercial anchor, with separately scoped:
- paid discovery and PoV
- implementation and data mapping
- named-system connector development
- annual support and maintenance
- optional premium support / SLA
- optional cloud hosting and usage capacity

For international selling, quote in USD or the customer's contracting currency, with tax, withholding, FX, hosting and travel treatment explicit. Use value-based pricing against customer-validated addressable revenue leakage and operational savings. Do not price against unverified gross exposure or promise a percentage of recovery without legal, procurement and regulatory review.

For customer-hosted deployments, charge annual licence by edition, modules, environment and capacity. For cloud, charge annual platform subscription with tenant, usage and retention tiers. Offer the same product capability across deployment modes, with hosting/infrastructure responsibilities clearly separated.

## Sales motion

1. Executive discovery: understand meter-to-cash process, revenue assurance pain, existing controls and business outcomes.
2. Data-readiness assessment: review source inventory, ownership, quality, time boundaries and legal basis.
3. Synthetic demonstration: walk through data ingestion, reconciliation, evidence, investigation, approval and outcome.
4. Paid PoV: agree 90-day scope, baseline, customer data access, success metrics, roles and exit criteria.
5. Evaluation: compare findings with customer-labelled cases and finance-confirmed outcomes.
6. Production proposal: deployment architecture, named integrations, security pack, licence schedule, support SLA and rollout plan.
7. Expansion: add service areas, business units, modules and other subsidiaries.

## PoV success measures

Agree baseline and denominator with the customer before customer-data evaluation:
- share of eligible records successfully ingested
- reconciliation coverage and unresolved exception rate
- reviewed-finding precision and false-positive burden
- median time to triage and close
- evidence completeness and auditability
- approved recovery versus cash actually received
- verified incremental recovery attributable to validated cases
- user adoption and reviewer override reasons

Do not publish accuracy, savings, recovery or ROI claims from synthetic data.

## Sales enablement pack

- Executive presentation and one-page value proposition
- Working synthetic PoV with repeatable scenarios and reset
- 90-day PoV scope, acceptance criteria and evaluation scorecard
- Security architecture, threat model and data-flow diagram
- Customer data dictionary and source-mapping workbook
- Deployment and integration questionnaire
- Licensing schedule, support SLA and statement of work
- Case study only after customer-approved, independently defensible results

## Worldwide readiness

Before selling in each jurisdiction, configure and review:
- currency, decimal precision, FX and tax rules
- tariff structures, effective dates and customer classes
- utility hierarchy, meter units and local time zones
- data protection, residency, retention and cross-border transfer rules
- critical infrastructure and cybersecurity obligations
- procurement, records and audit requirements
- local language, accessibility and support arrangements

Do not claim universal regulatory compliance or certification. Maintain a jurisdiction-specific legal and security applicability register.

## Partner strategy

Prioritise utility systems integrators, metering/AMI vendors, billing-system implementers, payment providers and local cybersecurity firms. Partner integrations must use documented APIs, least-privilege credentials, customer-approved scopes and formal support boundaries.

## 12-month commercial milestones (planning targets, not forecasts)

- Month 1–3: synthetic PoV, IBEDC discovery, data readiness and paid PoV proposal.
- Month 4–6: customer-data validation, independent security review and first referenceable outcome if customer approves publication.
- Month 7–9: reusable connector framework, second/third utility pursuits and local partner enablement.
- Month 10–12: international pilot pipeline, cloud tenancy hardening and enterprise support readiness.

Progress is gated by customer evidence, security review and acceptance; calendar milestones do not imply product certification.
